Hybrid Recurrent U-Net (aria-redefine-qbit)

This submission implements a Hybrid Recurrent U-Net Transformer designed to maximize expressive depth within the 16.7MB constraint. The architecture bifurcates the network into a unique Encoder stage and a Depth-Recurrent Decoder stage to decouple computational depth from parameter count.

Key Technical Pillars:

- Hybrid Decoder Recurrence: While the Encoder remains unique for specialized feature extraction, the Decoder utilizes a weight-shared looping mechanism. This allows the model to iteratively refine high-level semantics by re-processing encoder skip connections over multiple cycles, increasing "virtual" depth without increasing binary size.

- Entropy-Aware MLP Scaling: Empirical testing identified a compression-to-legality Pareto front at an MLP\_MULT of 1.9x. This configuration yielded 16.53MB (int8+zlib), providing a safety buffer while maintaining higher representational capacity than standard 1.0x configurations.

- Muon-Optimized Orthogonality: I utilized the Muon optimizer with @torch.compile Newton-Schulz kernels. This ensures internal rank stability during recurrent passes, preventing the gradient stagnation often seen in weight-shared architectures.
